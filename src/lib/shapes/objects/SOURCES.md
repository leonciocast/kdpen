# Object, food, nature and holiday silhouettes

Every silhouette in `objects.ts` is an original geometric construction
drawn for this project (circles, polygons, and simple radial unions).
They are not traced from commercial word-search or crossword books, from
stock illustration, or from any third-party SVG set.

The stepped block letters and digits in `stepped.ts` are also original
7-row forms designed so a scaled stroke can hold words.

If a future shape is taken from a public-domain or CC0 source, record
the URL, author, and license here, the same way animal silhouettes are
credited in `src/lib/shapes/animals/SOURCES.md`.
